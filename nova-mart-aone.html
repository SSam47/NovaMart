Why itworks.

How does it stop ash from flying around the car?

The top opening directs falling ash straight into the inner chamber. Even with open windows or active air conditioning, ash stays contained inside.

Slide the cigarette into the silicone collar. The chamber holds butts securely and limits smoke and odor until you empty it.

Does it fit standard car cup holders and bags?
